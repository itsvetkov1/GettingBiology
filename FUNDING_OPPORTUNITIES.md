**Last Updated**: January 17, 2026

## Executive Summary

This document tracks active funding opportunities in Bulgaria and the EU for the GettingBiology educational app. Focus areas: educational technology, digital skills, youth entrepreneurship, and STEM education.

## 💼 Medium-Term Opportunities (2026)

### Economic Transformation Programme
- **Budget**: EUR 690 million
- **Deadline**: June 30, 2026 implementation
- **Three Funds**:
  1. Growth and Innovation ⭐ (Best fit)
  2. Green Transition
  3. Climate
- **Eligibility**: SMEs and mid-caps
- **Action**: Contact Ministry of Innovation and Growth for open calls

### Green and Digital Partnerships (BG16RFPR002-1.010)
- **Target**: SMEs with research partnerships
- **Requirement**: Partner with university or research institution
- **Strategy**:
  - Contact Sofia University Biology Department
  - Propose educational technology research collaboration
  - Develop data-driven learning effectiveness study
- **Action**: Draft partnership proposal for Bulgarian universities

### Horizon Europe 2026-2027
- **Budget**: €14 billion (35% for digital)
- **Portal**: EU Funding and Tenders Portal
- **Best Fit Calls**:
  - Digital education innovation
  - STEM learning technologies
  - Educational equity and access
- **Action**: Monitor open calls quarterly at https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders
